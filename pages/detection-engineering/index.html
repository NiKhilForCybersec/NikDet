Detection Engineering

The discipline of creating, testing, and maintaining high-fidelity security detections that surface real threats while minimizing false positives.

The Detection Engineering Process

1
Identify Threat

Research adversary techniques, review threat intel, understand the behavior to detect.

2
Understand Data

Identify which log sources capture the behavior and what fields are available.

3
Develop Detection

Write the query/rule, accounting for variations and edge cases.

4
Test & Validate

Verify detection fires for true positives and doesn't create excessive false positives.

5
Deploy & Monitor

Push to production, monitor performance, gather analyst feedback.

6
Tune & Iterate

Refine based on false positive feedback, add exclusions, improve fidelity.

Key Metrics

  • True Positive Rate: % of alerts that are real (target: >70%)
  • False Positive Rate: % of alerts that are noise (target: <30%)
  • Detection Latency: Time from event to alert
  • Coverage Score: % of MITRE techniques with detections
  • Detection Velocity: New detections deployed per month
