Common Level 2 Mistakes

  • Ignoring alert tuning: High FP rates burn out analysts and cause alert fatigue
  • No documentation: Knowledge leaves when people leave the team
  • Tool hoarding: Buying tools without staff to properly use them
  • Skipping basics: Jumping to advanced capabilities before fundamentals are solid
  • No metrics: Can't improve what you don't measure - track KPIs from day one
  • Isolation: Not building relationships with IT, management, and business units

Level 2 Success Indicators

  • Security alerts are reviewed same business day
  • Playbooks exist for top 5 alert types
  • Monthly incident report delivered to management
  • True positive rate improving month over month
  • No major incidents discovered by external parties first
