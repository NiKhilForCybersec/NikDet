Crown Jewels Analysis

Identify your organization's most critical assets - the ones that would cause catastrophic damage if compromised. Focus detection and protection on what matters most.

What Are Crown Jewels?

Crown jewels are assets whose compromise would result in:

  • Existential business impact: Could shut down the company
  • Major financial loss: Millions in direct loss or fines
  • Severe reputational damage: Loss of customer trust
  • Regulatory action: License revocation, sanctions
  • Competitive disadvantage: Loss of trade secrets

Crown Jewel Identification Process

1
Business Impact Analysis

Interview business leaders: "What would shut us down? What keeps you up at night?"

2
Data Classification Review

Review data classification scheme. What's classified as highest sensitivity?

3
Regulatory Mapping

What data is subject to GDPR, HIPAA, PCI, SOX? Penalties for breach?

4
Asset Dependencies

Map which systems host/process/transmit crown jewel data.

5
Access Path Mapping

Who/what has access to crown jewels? What's the attack path?

Crown Jewel Detection Queries

// Access to crown jewel systems
let CrownJewels = datatable(System:string)["DC01", "SQLPROD", "PAYMENT01"];
SecurityEvent
| where TimeGenerated > ago(24h)
| where Computer in (CrownJewels)
| where EventID in (4624, 4625, 4648)
| project TimeGenerated, Computer, Account, EventID, LogonType, IpAddress

// Database queries on sensitive tables
AuditLogs
| where TimeGenerated > ago(24h)
| where TargetResources contains "CustomerPII" or TargetResources contains "Payment"
| project TimeGenerated, InitiatedBy, OperationName, TargetResources

// Bulk data access patterns
OfficeActivity
| where TimeGenerated > ago(24h)
| where Site_Url contains "Confidential" or Site_Url contains "Restricted"
| summarize FileCount=count(), Files=make_set(SourceFileName) by UserId
| where FileCount > 50

Crown Jewel Protection Checklist

  • ☐ Crown jewels documented and approved by leadership
  • ☐ All systems hosting crown jewels identified
  • ☐ Network segmentation implemented
  • ☐ Enhanced logging enabled
  • ☐ Privileged access restricted and monitored
  • ☐ Dedicated detection rules deployed
  • ☐ Faster SLA for crown jewel alerts
  • ☐ Regular access reviews conducted
  • ☐ Backup and recovery tested
