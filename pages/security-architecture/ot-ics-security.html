Safety-Critical Considerations
  • Never isolate Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) without proper procedures
  • Coordinate all containment with plant operations
  • Prioritize physical safety over cyber incident response
  • Have vendor support on standby for critical systems
  • Know manual override procedures for all automated systems
