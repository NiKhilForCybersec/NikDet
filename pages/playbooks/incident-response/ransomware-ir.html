PB-IR-001

Ransomware Incident Response

Emergency response procedures for active ransomware incidents.

SeverityCRITICAL
TierIR Team
Time Critical

Minutes matter. Every moment of delay allows further encryption and lateral movement.

Immediate Actions (First 15 Minutes)

PriorityActionWho
1Isolate known infected systems - Network contain via EDR or switchSOC
2DO NOT power off systems - Memory contains keys/artifactsAll
3Disable compromised accounts - Stop lateral movementIAM Team
4Block known C2 - Firewall/DNS blockNetwork Team
5Activate IR team - All hands on deckIR Lead
6Notify leadership - CISO, CIO, LegalIR Lead

Containment Phase

  • Network segmentation: Isolate affected segments
  • Disable admin shares: Stop SMB-based spread
  • Disconnect backups: Protect backup infrastructure
  • Block lateral movement: Restrict RDP, WMI, PsExec
  • Preserve evidence: Memory dumps, disk images

Investigation Phase

  • Identify ransomware variant (ID Ransomware, ransom note)
  • Determine scope (how many systems affected)
  • Find patient zero (first infected system)
  • Identify entry vector (phishing, RDP, vulnerability)
  • Check for data exfiltration (double extortion)
  • Timeline the attack (how long were they in?)

Recovery Phase

OptionWhen to Use
Restore from backupBackups available, verified clean
Decryption toolKnown variant with available decryptor
Rebuild systemsNo backups, faster to rebuild
Negotiate/pay (last resort)Critical data, no alternatives

Post-Incident

  • Root cause analysis
  • Remediate entry vector
  • Lessons learned documentation
  • Update detection rules
  • Consider regulatory notification
  • Enhance backup strategy
