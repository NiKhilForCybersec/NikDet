Risk Assessment Methodology

Structured approaches to identifying, analyzing, and prioritizing security risks.

Risk Assessment Process

  1. Scope Definition - Define boundaries and objectives
  2. Asset Identification - Inventory critical assets
  3. Threat Identification - Identify potential threats
  4. Vulnerability Assessment - Find weaknesses
  5. Risk Analysis - Calculate likelihood × impact
  6. Risk Evaluation - Prioritize risks
  7. Treatment Planning - Accept, mitigate, transfer, avoid
  8. Documentation & Reporting - Document findings

Risk Calculation

Basic Risk Formula

Risk = Likelihood × Impact

Or more detailed: Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Asset Value

Risk Treatment Options

OptionDescriptionWhen to Use
AcceptAccept the risk as-isLow risk, cost of mitigation > benefit
MitigateImplement controls to reduce riskMost common approach
TransferTransfer risk to third partyInsurance, outsourcing
AvoidEliminate the risk sourceUnacceptable risk, stop the activity
