Alert Investigation FAQ

Q: The alert fired but I can't find any related logs. What do I do?

Possible causes and solutions:

  • Log retention: Logs may have aged out. Check your retention period.
  • Time zone mismatch: Alert time may be in different timezone than logs. Convert to UTC.
  • Ingestion delay: Logs may not be ingested yet. Wait 5-15 minutes and retry.
  • Log source not configured: The relevant log source may not be sending to SIEM.
  • Query error: Double-check your search syntax and field names.
  • Wrong index: You may be searching the wrong index/table.

Q: How do I know if an alert is a true positive or false positive?

Questions to ask yourself:

  • Is the activity expected for this user/system role?
  • Does the timing make sense (business hours, user's timezone)?
  • Is there a change ticket or maintenance window?
  • Have we seen this pattern before (check historical alerts)?
  • Does the user/system have a legitimate reason?
  • Are there corroborating indicators from other sources?

Q: The user says they didn't do it. How do I verify?

Verification steps:

  • Check the source IP - is it the user's usual location/device?
  • Check device name - is it their assigned device?
  • Check timing - were they working at that time?
  • Check for impossible travel - were they elsewhere at the time?
  • Check for MFA completion - did they complete MFA?
  • Check browser/user agent - does it match their usual?

Q: I found malware but it was blocked. Can I close the ticket?

Not so fast! Check for:

  • Was this the only detection or are there more?
  • How did the malware get there (email, download, USB)?
  • Are other systems affected?
  • Did anything execute before blocking?
  • Is there any C2 communication in network logs?
  • Should you search for IOCs across the environment?

When to Escalate

Always escalate if:

  • Ransomware indicators (even if blocked)
  • Active C2 communication
  • Credential theft confirmed (LSASS access, Mimikatz)
  • Multiple systems affected
  • VIP/executive account involved
  • Critical infrastructure affected
  • You're not sure and it could be bad
  • Evidence of data exfiltration
  • Attacker has achieved persistence
  • You need permissions you don't have
