Log Analysis

Understanding logs is the foundation of security operations. Learn to read what systems are telling you.

Why Log Analysis Matters

Logs are the memory of your systems. Every action, every connection, every change leaves a trace. As a SOC analyst, your job is to find the malicious signals hidden in the noise of normal activity.

Key Principles
  • Know your baseline: Understand what normal looks like
  • Context is king: A single log rarely tells the whole story
  • Correlate across sources: Connect events across systems
  • Time matters: Sequence of events reveals intent

Log Sources by Platform

Windows Event Logs

Security, System, Application, and specialized logs. The core of Windows security monitoring.

Sysmon

Enhanced Windows logging for process, network, file, and registry activity. Essential for detection engineering.

Linux Logs

Syslog, auditd, auth.log, and application logs. Critical for server and cloud infrastructure.

Network Logs

Firewall, proxy, DNS, and flow logs. Visibility into network communications and lateral movement.

Log Analysis Process

1
Identify Relevant Data Sources

Based on your hypothesis or alert, determine which logs contain relevant information. Different attack types require different log sources.

2
Extract and Filter

Query the data with appropriate filters. Start broad and narrow down. Use time ranges, specific fields, and patterns to reduce noise.

3
Analyze and Correlate

Look for patterns, anomalies, and connections between events. Correlate across multiple log sources to build a complete picture.

4
Document and Conclude

Record your findings with evidence. Draw conclusions based on the data and recommend next steps.

Tips for Effective Analysis

  • Start with time: Anchor your search around known-good timestamps
  • Use aggregations: Stats, counts, and groupings reveal patterns
  • Look for outliers: Rare events often indicate suspicious activity
  • Pivot on findings: When you find something, dig deeper
  • Document your queries: Save successful searches for reuse
