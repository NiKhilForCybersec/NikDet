Learning Path for New Analysts

1
Week 1-2: Fundamentals

Security concepts, CIA triad, common attacks, basic networking, Windows/Linux basics.

2
Week 3-4: Tools

SIEM navigation, basic queries, EDR console, ticketing system, asset inventory.

3
Week 5-6: Processes

Playbooks, escalation procedures, shift handoff, documentation standards.

4
Week 7-8: Shadowing

Work alongside experienced analysts, handle simple alerts with guidance and feedback.

5
Month 3+: Independence

Handle alerts independently, continue learning advanced topics, seek mentorship.

Success Tips for New Analysts

  • Be curious: Always ask "why" and dig deeper into alerts
  • Build relationships: Know your IT team, asset owners, and management
  • Stay current: Follow threat intel, read security news daily
  • Practice outside work: Home labs, CTFs, courses strengthen skills
  • Document everything: Build your personal knowledge base
  • Seek feedback: Ask seniors to review your work regularly
  • Embrace mistakes: Every analyst makes them - learn and improve

Foundation Building Checklist

  • ☐ Complete Security+ or equivalent training
  • ☐ Master your SIEM's basic query language
  • ☐ Understand your organization's network topology
  • ☐ Know the top 10 alert types and their playbooks
  • ☐ Build rapport with L2/L3 analysts for mentorship
  • ☐ Set up a home lab for practice
  • ☐ Complete one CTF challenge per month
