PB-IR-005

Insider Threat Incident Response

Response procedures for confirmed insider threat incidents requiring coordination with HR and Legal.

SensitivityCONFIDENTIAL
Legal/HR Coordination Required

All insider threat investigations MUST be coordinated with Legal and HR. Do NOT take containment actions without approval. Do NOT confront the subject.

Insider Threat Categories

CategoryIntentExamples
MaliciousIntentional harmData theft, sabotage, fraud
NegligentUnintentionalPolicy violations, accidental exposure
CompromisedAccount takeoverCredentials stolen by external actor
DepartingTaking data on way outCopying before resignation/termination

Key Stakeholders

StakeholderRoleWhen to Engage
HREmployee relations, disciplinary actionImmediately
LegalLegal implications, evidence handlingImmediately
ManagementBusiness decisions, communicationsAfter HR/Legal consultation
Physical SecurityBadge access, building accessIf termination planned

Response by Scenario

ScenarioUrgencyResponse
Active data theft in progressImmediateMay need immediate account disable (get approval first)
Historical data theft discoveredModeratePreserve evidence, assess scope, plan with HR/Legal
Policy violationLowHR handles, Security documents
Departing employee riskModerateEnhanced monitoring, controlled offboarding

Evidence Preservation

  • Legal hold: Preserve all email, files, and communications
  • Forensic image: Device imaging if warranted (coordinate with Legal)
  • Log preservation: Extend retention beyond normal period
  • Chain of custody: Document all evidence handling
  • Screenshots: Capture evidence that may change (cloud files, permissions)

Investigation Focus Areas

  • What data was accessed, copied, or exfiltrated?
  • When did the suspicious activity begin?
  • What methods were used? (USB, email, cloud, print)
  • Where did the data go? (personal email, cloud storage, competitor?)
  • What is the potential business impact?
  • Was anyone else involved?

Critical Rules

  • Never confront the subject directly
  • All communications through HR and Legal
  • Document everything but limit distribution
  • Need-to-know basis for investigation details
  • Assume everything could be used in court
