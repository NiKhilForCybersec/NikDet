Enterprise Risk Assessment

Complete risk assessment methodology with industry-specific examples. Covers asset identification, threat modeling, vulnerability assessment, and risk treatment across different organizational types.

Risk Assessment Methodology

Risk Calculation Formula

Risk Formula

Risk = Likelihood × Impact × Asset Value

Where: Likelihood (1-5), Impact (1-5), Asset Value (1-5)

Risk Score Range: 1-125 | Critical: >75 | High: 50-75 | Medium: 25-49 | Low: <25

Likelihood Rating Scale

RatingLikelihoodFrequencyDescription
