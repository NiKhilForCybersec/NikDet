Daily Operations Workflow

1
Shift Handover

Review open tickets, ongoing incidents, and priorities from previous shift.

2
Queue Management

Work through alert queue by priority, following playbooks for each type.

3
Investigation

Investigate alerts, gather evidence, determine true/false positive.

4
Response

Take containment actions, escalate as needed, document findings.

5
Closure

Document resolution, update tickets, provide recommendations.
