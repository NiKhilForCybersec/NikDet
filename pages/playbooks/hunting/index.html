Hunting Playbooks

Proactive threat hunting procedures organized by MITRE ATT&CK tactics. Find threats that evade automated detection.

Hunting Philosophy

Core Principle: Assume compromise. Hunters proactively search for evidence of threats that have bypassed existing defenses.

Hunting Playbooks by ATT&CK Tactic

Initial Access

Phishing, exploits, valid accounts.

Persistence

Registry, scheduled tasks, services.

Privilege Escalation

Token manipulation, UAC bypass.

Defense Evasion

Log clearing, masquerading, disabling security.

Credential Access

Credential dumping, Kerberoasting.

Lateral Movement

RDP, PsExec, WMI, remote services.

Exfiltration

Data staging, transfer, C2 channels.

Hunt Outputs

  • New Detection Rules: Convert successful hunts into automated detections
  • Visibility Gaps: Identify missing log sources or telemetry
  • Baseline Updates: Refine understanding of "normal"
  • Threat Intel: Internal IOCs and TTPs observed
  • Incident Discovery: Previously undetected compromises
