Certification Deep Dives

Entry Level (Start Here)

🟢 CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)

Cost:~$400 exam
Difficulty:Moderate
Study time:2-3 months
Validity:3 years

Why get it: Industry standard entry cert. Required by many job postings. DoD 8570 approved.

What you learn: Broad security fundamentals - threats, architecture, operations, GRC.

Study resources: Professor Messer (free), Jason Dion Udemy, CompTIA CertMaster

🟢 CompTIA CySA+ (CS0-003)

Cost:~$400 exam
Difficulty:Moderate-Hard
Study time:2-3 months
Validity:3 years

Why get it: More SOC-focused than Security+. Shows analyst-specific skills.

What you learn: Threat detection, analysis, vulnerability management, incident response.

Study resources: Jason Dion, TryHackMe SOC path, practice labs

🟢 Blue Team Level 1 (BTL1)

Cost:~$400
Difficulty:Moderate
Study time:1-2 months
Validity:Lifetime

Why get it: Hands-on practical exam. Shows you can actually DO SOC work.

What you learn: SIEM analysis, phishing analysis, threat intel, digital forensics basics.

Study resources: Security Blue Team training included

Intermediate (Build Expertise)

🟡 Microsoft SC-200

Cost:~$165 exam
Difficulty:Moderate
Study time:1-2 months
Validity:1 year (free renewal)

Why get it: If you use Microsoft Sentinel/MDE, this is essential. Cheap and valuable.

What you learn: KQL, Sentinel, MDE, Microsoft 365 Defender, investigation techniques.

Study resources: Microsoft Learn (free), John Savill YouTube

🟡 GIAC GCIH (Incident Handler)

Cost:~$2,500 (with SANS training: $8,000+)
Difficulty:Hard
Study time:2-3 months
Validity:4 years

Why get it: Gold standard for incident response. Highly respected industry-wide.

What you learn: Incident handling process, attack techniques, defense strategies.

Tips: Try to get employer to pay. SANS training is excellent but expensive.

Advanced (Specialize)

🔴 OSCP (Offensive Security)

Cost:~$1,600+ (with lab access)
Difficulty:Very Hard
Study time:3-6 months
Validity:Lifetime

Why get it: Proves hands-on hacking skills. Highly respected for red team/pentest roles.

What you learn: Penetration testing methodology, exploit development, privilege escalation.

Note: 24-hour practical exam. You either hack the machines or you fail.

🔴 CISSP

Cost:~$750 exam
Difficulty:Hard (breadth, not depth)
Study time:2-4 months
Prerequisite:5 years experience

Why get it: Required for many senior/management roles. Shows broad security knowledge.

What you learn: 8 domains covering security management, architecture, operations.

Note: Management-focused. Not technical depth, but breadth.

🎯 Getting Employer to Pay

Many employers have training budgets. Here's how to get them to pay:

  • Ask! Many people never ask and leave money on the table.
  • Frame it as business value: "This cert will help me do X better"
  • Propose a retention agreement: "I'll stay 1 year after certification"
  • Time it with performance reviews or budget cycles

Key Takeaways

The Bottom Line

  • Start with Security+ - it's the industry baseline
  • Add SOC-specific certs - CySA+, SC-200, BTL1
  • Get vendor certs for your tools - matches your job
  • GCIH is gold standard - worth the investment for IR
  • Experience + Cert > Cert alone - always
  • Get employer to pay when possible - always ask
