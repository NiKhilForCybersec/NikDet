Level 4: Measured

Data-driven security operations with extensive automation, continuous improvement cycles, and deep threat intelligence integration.

Level 4 Characteristics

DomainTypical State
BusinessSecurity integrated into business decisions, risk-based prioritization
PeopleSpecialized roles (hunters, engineers), continuous learning culture
ProcessMetrics-driven optimization, formal change management
TechnologyFull SOAR automation, ML-enhanced detection, API integration
ServicesProactive services dominate, intelligence-led operations

Level 4 Capabilities

  • Advanced automation: 70%+ of Tier 1 alerts auto-handled
  • Threat hunting program: Regular hypothesis-driven hunts
  • Detection engineering: Dedicated team, detection-as-code
  • Intelligence operations: Produce and consume threat intel
  • Metrics maturity: Predictive analytics, trend analysis
  • Purple teaming: Regular adversary emulation exercises

Common Level 4 Challenges

  • Diminishing returns on additional investment
  • Complexity management across integrated systems
  • Keeping automation current with changing environment
  • Measuring value of proactive activities
  • Talent competition with well-funded adversaries

Advanced Operational Metrics

  • Mean time to contain - Speed of threat neutralization
  • Detection gap analysis - Known blind spots quantified
  • Automation ROI - Hours saved, cost avoided
  • Hunt effectiveness - Unique threats found per hunt
  • Intel actionability - % of intel that drives action
