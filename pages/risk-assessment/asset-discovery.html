Asset Discovery

You can't protect what you don't know exists. Asset discovery is the foundation of effective security operations and risk management.

Why Asset Discovery Matters

  • Unknown assets can't be patched, monitored, or protected
  • Shadow IT creates blind spots for attackers to exploit
  • Compliance requires knowing what systems handle regulated data
  • Incident response requires knowing your environment
  • Risk assessment requires complete asset inventory

Discovery Process

1
Define Scope

Identify all network ranges, cloud accounts, and business units to inventory.

2
Choose Discovery Methods

Select appropriate techniques for each environment type.

3
Execute Discovery

Run scans, collect data from agents and APIs, gather manual inputs.

4
Normalize & Deduplicate

Merge data sources, resolve conflicts, create single source of truth.

5
Enrich & Classify

Add business context, assign owners, classify criticality.

6
Maintain Continuously

Schedule regular scans, automate updates, monitor for new assets.

Common Tools

  • Network scanning: Nmap, Masscan, Rumble
  • Vulnerability scanners: Nessus, Qualys, Rapid7
  • Cloud: AWS Config, Azure Resource Graph, GCP Asset Inventory
  • Endpoint: SCCM, Intune, Jamf, EDR platforms
  • CMDB: ServiceNow, BMC, custom solutions
