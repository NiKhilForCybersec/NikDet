SOC Maturity Model

Assess and improve your Security Operations Center capabilities through structured maturity levels based on the SOC-CMM framework.

Why Maturity Assessment Matters

Understanding your SOC's current maturity level helps you identify gaps, prioritize improvements, and demonstrate progress to leadership. A mature SOC doesn't happen overnight—it requires deliberate investment in people, processes, and technology across multiple capability domains.

The Five Maturity Levels

Level 1: Initial

Ad-hoc processes, reactive response, limited visibility. The starting point for most organizations.

Level 2: Managed

Basic processes documented, some tooling in place, beginning to track metrics.

Level 3: Defined

Standardized processes, integrated tools, proactive threat hunting, formal training.

Level 4: Measured

Data-driven decisions, automation, continuous improvement, threat intelligence integration.

Level 5: Optimizing

Industry-leading capabilities, innovation, predictive security, full automation.

SOC-CMM Capability Domains

The SOC Capability Maturity Model evaluates organizations across five key domains:

Maturity Assessment Approach

1
Self-Assessment

Evaluate current capabilities against each maturity level's criteria. Be honest about gaps.

2
Gap Analysis

Identify the delta between current state and target state for each capability domain.

3
Prioritization

Focus on high-impact improvements that align with business risk and available resources.

4
Roadmap Development

Create a phased plan with milestones, resource requirements, and success metrics.

5
Continuous Improvement

Reassess regularly (annually minimum) and adjust roadmap based on progress and changing threats.
