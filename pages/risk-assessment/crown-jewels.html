Crown Jewels Analysis

Identify and protect your organization's most critical assets.

What Are Crown Jewels?

Crown jewels are the assets that, if compromised, would cause the most significant damage to the organization. They're the primary targets attackers want to reach.

Common Crown Jewels by Industry

IndustryTypical Crown Jewels
Financial ServicesCustomer PII, trading algorithms, transaction systems, SWIFT
HealthcarePatient records (PHI), medical devices, research data
TechnologySource code, customer data, IP, infrastructure secrets
ManufacturingProduct designs, supply chain data, OT/ICS systems
RetailCustomer data, payment systems, inventory systems
GovernmentClassified data, citizen data, critical infrastructure

Identifying Crown Jewels

Questions to Ask

  • What data would cause regulatory fines if breached?
  • What systems would halt operations if unavailable?
  • What intellectual property would competitors value?
  • What would cause the most reputational damage?
  • What do attackers typically target in our industry?

Criticality Criteria

CriteriaDescription
Financial ImpactDirect cost of loss (fines, lawsuits, lost revenue)
Operational ImpactEffect on business operations if unavailable
Regulatory ImpactCompliance violations and associated penalties
Reputational ImpactDamage to brand and customer trust
Strategic ImpactEffect on competitive advantage

Protection Strategies

  • Enhanced monitoring: More detection rules, lower thresholds
  • Stronger access controls: MFA, privileged access management
  • Network segmentation: Isolate from general network
  • Encryption: At rest and in transit
  • Backup and recovery: Tested, offline backups
  • Incident response priority: Crown jewel incidents get top attention

SOC Implications

  • Dedicated detection rules: Custom detections for crown jewel systems
  • Elevated alert severity: Any alert on crown jewel = higher priority
  • 24/7 coverage: Critical assets monitored around the clock
  • Faster response SLAs: Shorter response times for crown jewel incidents
  • Executive notification: Immediate escalation for confirmed incidents
