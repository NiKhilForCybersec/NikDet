Threat Modeling

Systematically identify threats and attack vectors to improve defenses.

What is Threat Modeling?

Threat modeling is a structured approach to identifying potential threats, understanding attack vectors, and prioritizing security measures. It answers: "What could go wrong?"

Common Frameworks

FrameworkFocusBest For
STRIDE Threat categories (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Info Disclosure, DoS, Elevation) Application security, design review
MITRE ATT&CK Adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures Detection engineering, threat hunting
PASTA Process for Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis Risk-centric analysis
Kill Chain Attack phases (Recon → Weaponize → Deliver → Exploit → Install → C2 → Actions) Understanding attack progression

Threat Modeling Process

1
Define Scope

What system/application are we modeling? What are the boundaries?

2
Identify Assets & Entry Points

What are we protecting? How can attackers get in?

3
Identify Threats

Who might attack? What are their goals and capabilities?

4
Identify Attack Vectors

How could each threat be realized? Map using ATT&CK.

5
Prioritize & Mitigate

Rank threats by risk. Identify controls and detection gaps.

SOC Application

  • Detection priorities: Build detections for likely attack vectors
  • Hunt hypotheses: Use threat models to guide hunting
  • Coverage analysis: Map detections to modeled threats
  • Tabletop exercises: Use scenarios for IR practice
  • Alert tuning: Understand which threats justify more noise
