The Wake-Up Call

🚨 The Incident: March 2024

Three months ago, Apex experienced a security incident that exposed gaps in their detection capabilities:

INCIDENT SUMMARY: BEC Attack Leading to Wire Fraud

Timeline:
- Day 1: CFO received phishing email (bypassed email security)
- Day 1: CFO clicked link, entered credentials on fake O365 page
- Day 2-14: Attacker monitored CFO's email silently
- Day 14: Attacker created inbox rule forwarding payment-related emails
- Day 21: Attacker sent spoofed email as "vendor" with new bank details
- Day 22: Finance wired $340,000 to fraudulent account
- Day 23: Real vendor called asking about payment - fraud discovered

Detection Failures:
❌ Phishing email not detected (new domain, no reputation)
❌ Credential theft not detected (no impossible travel alert)
❌ Inbox rule creation not detected (no monitoring)
❌ Unusual email access patterns not detected
❌ Wire transfer to new account not flagged

How It Was Discovered:
- Legitimate vendor called asking where their payment was
- NOT detected by security tools

Impact:

  • $340,000 lost (partially recovered through bank)
  • SEC notification required
  • Board demanded security improvements
  • CISO given budget for detection engineering program

Threat Assessment

Who Would Target Apex?

🔴 HIGH RISK: Financially Motivated Criminals

Motivation: Direct financial gain through BEC, ransomware, or data theft

Capability: Moderate - use commodity tools, phishing kits

Likelihood: HIGH - already happened once

Primary TTPs:

  • Phishing for credentials (T1566)
  • Business Email Compromise (T1534)
  • Ransomware deployment (T1486)

🟡 MEDIUM RISK: Insider Threats

Motivation: Financial gain, revenge, competitor espionage

Capability: High - legitimate access, knows systems

Likelihood: MEDIUM - financial industry has higher insider risk

Primary TTPs:

  • Data exfiltration to personal accounts (T1567)
  • Privilege abuse (T1078)
  • Access to client data before resignation

🟡 MEDIUM RISK: Nation-State (Economic Espionage)

Motivation: Market intelligence, client information

Capability: Very High - advanced tools, patient

Likelihood: LOW-MEDIUM - not primary target but financial sector is targeted

Primary TTPs:

  • Spearphishing with zero-days (T1566.001)
  • Supply chain compromise (T1195)
  • Long-term persistence (T1053, T1543)

Detection Priorities

Based on the assessment, here's the detection engineering roadmap:

1
Month 1: BEC Prevention (Post-Incident)

Email rule monitoring, impossible travel, OAuth app consent, MFA bypass detection

2
Month 2: Identity Protection

Credential dumping, Kerberoasting, Pass-the-Hash, privileged account abuse

3
Month 3: Endpoint Threats

Ransomware indicators, LOLBin abuse, persistence mechanisms, lateral movement

4
Month 4: Data Protection

Large file transfers, cloud storage uploads, unusual database queries, insider threat indicators

5
Month 5-6: Infrastructure Gaps

Linux server monitoring, AWS integration, application logging improvements
