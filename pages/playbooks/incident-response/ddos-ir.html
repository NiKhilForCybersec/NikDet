PB-IR-004

DDoS Incident Response

Response procedures for Distributed Denial of Service attacks.

SeverityHIGH

DDoS Attack Types

TypeLayerDescriptionMitigation
VolumetricL3/L4UDP flood, ICMP flood, amplificationUpstream scrubbing, null routing
ProtocolL3/L4SYN flood, fragmentation attacksSYN cookies, rate limiting
ApplicationL7HTTP flood, Slowloris, DNS query floodWAF, bot detection, CAPTCHA
Multi-vectorMixedCombination of aboveLayered defense

Immediate Response (First 15 Minutes)

StepActionWho
1Confirm attack - Distinguish from legitimate traffic spikeNOC/SOC
2Activate DDoS protection - Enable scrubbing serviceNetwork Team
3Contact ISP - Request upstream filteringNetwork Team
4Enable CDN protection - "Under Attack" modeNetwork Team
5Rate limit aggressively - Temporary strict limitsNetwork Team
6Notify stakeholders - Management, communicationsIR Lead

Analysis During Attack

  • Characterize the attack: Type, volume, vectors, duration
  • Identify source IPs: Ranges, geographic distribution
  • Capture traffic samples: PCAP for analysis and evidence
  • Monitor service availability: Track uptime and degradation
  • Track attack evolution: Attackers often adapt tactics

Mitigation Techniques

TechniqueDescriptionWhen to Use
Geo-blockingBlock traffic from certain countriesAttack from non-business regions
Rate limitingLimit requests per IP/sessionApplication layer attacks
CAPTCHA/JS challengeFilter out botsHTTP floods from botnets
Blackhole routingDrop all traffic to target IPLast resort, sacrifices availability
Scrubbing serviceRoute traffic through cleaning centerLarge volumetric attacks

Communication Plan

  • Status page: Update public status page if customer-facing
  • Internal: Regular updates to leadership and affected teams
  • Customers: Proactive notification if service impacted
  • Media: Prepared statement through communications team

Post-Attack Actions

  • Document attack timeline and characteristics
  • Review protection effectiveness
  • Update runbooks and response procedures
  • Consider enhanced DDoS protection investment
  • Review and test failover capabilities
  • Conduct lessons learned session
