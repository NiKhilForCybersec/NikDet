The Risk Equation

Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact

ComponentDefinitionExample
ThreatActor or event that could cause harmRansomware groups, nation-states, insider
VulnerabilityWeakness that could be exploitedUnpatched CVE, misconfiguration, weak password
ImpactConsequence if threat exploits vulnerabilityData breach, downtime, financial loss

Risk Treatment Options

OptionDescriptionWhen to Use
MitigateImplement controls to reduce riskRisk is unacceptable, controls are cost-effective
AcceptAcknowledge and document riskCost of mitigation exceeds potential loss
TransferShift risk to third partyInsurance, outsourcing, contracts
AvoidEliminate the risk entirelyStop risky activity, remove system

Risk Assessment Process

1
Asset Identification

Inventory all assets: systems, data, people, processes.

2
Threat Identification

Determine relevant threats based on industry, geography, and profile.

3
Vulnerability Assessment

Identify weaknesses through scanning, testing, and review.

4
Impact Analysis

Determine business impact of potential security events.

5
Risk Calculation & Prioritization

Score and rank risks to focus remediation efforts.
