Level 3: Defined

Standardized processes across the organization, proactive threat hunting, and integrated tooling. The SOC operates as a professional security function.

Level 3 Characteristics

DomainTypical State
BusinessSOC has defined mission, reports to security leadership
PeopleTiered structure (L1/L2/L3), formal training program
ProcessComprehensive playbook library, defined SLAs
TechnologyIntegrated SIEM/SOAR/EDR, threat intel feeds
Services24x7 monitoring, proactive hunting, regular reporting

Common Level 3 Challenges

  • Scaling operations as organization grows
  • Keeping up with evolving threat landscape
  • Balancing proactive work with reactive queue
  • Tool integration complexity
  • Retaining skilled analysts
  • Demonstrating ROI to leadership

Priority Improvements to Reach Level 4

PriorityActionWhy It Matters
1Implement SOAR for repetitive tasksScale without adding headcount
2Establish threat hunting programFind threats that bypass detections
3Create detection engineering functionContinuously improve detection coverage
4Implement comprehensive metrics programData-driven decision making
5Integrate threat intelligence operationallyStay ahead of threats
6Develop career progression pathsRetain talent

Advanced Metrics to Track

  • Detection coverage - % of MITRE ATT&CK techniques covered
  • Dwell time - How long threats persist before detection
  • Automation rate - % of alerts auto-enriched/resolved
  • Threat intel utilization - IOCs operationalized
  • Hunt findings - Threats found proactively

Signs You're Ready for Level 4

  • Automation handles routine tasks
  • Regular threat hunting produces findings
  • Detection engineering is continuous process
  • Comprehensive metrics inform decisions
  • Threat intel integrated into operations
