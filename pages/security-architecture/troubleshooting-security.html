General Troubleshooting Approach

Universal Troubleshooting Steps
StepActionQuestions to Ask
1. Define the problemGet specific details about what's not workingWhat exactly is happening? What should happen? When did it start? What changed?
2. ReproduceCan you make it happen consistently?Does it happen every time? Specific users/systems? Specific times?
3. Check logsFind relevant log entriesWhat do logs show at time of issue? Any errors? What's the full error message?
4. Isolate variablesNarrow down the causeDoes it work for other users? Other systems? Other networks? Other applications?
5. Test hypothesisMake one change at a timeIf I change X, does the problem go away? Does it come back when I revert?
6. DocumentRecord what you foundWhat was the root cause? What fixed it? How do we prevent it?
