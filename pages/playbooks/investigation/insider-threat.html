Insider Threat Investigation

Investigating potential malicious or negligent insider activity - a sensitive investigation requiring careful handling.

Why This Investigation Matters

The Insider Threat Reality

Insiders have legitimate access, know where valuable data is, and understand security controls. They're the hardest threats to detect and can cause the most damage.

Insider TypeMotivationTypical ActionsDamage Potential
MaliciousFinancial gain, revenge, ideologyData theft, sabotage, fraudVery High
NegligentConvenience, ignorancePolicy violations, mishandling dataMedium-High
CompromisedCoerced, credentials stolenUsed as attack vectorHigh
DepartingTake work product, competitive advantageMass data download, IP theftHigh

Critical Investigation Considerations

IMPORTANT: Legal and HR Coordination

Insider threat investigations are NOT like other security investigations.

  • Involve HR and Legal EARLY - Before extensive investigation
  • Document everything - May be used in legal proceedings
  • Maintain confidentiality - Do not discuss with colleagues
  • Avoid tipping off the subject - Don't alert them to investigation
  • Follow company policy - Every organization has specific requirements
  • Consider privacy laws - GDPR, local regulations may apply

What NOT to Do

Don'tWhyInstead
Confront the employee yourselfCould compromise investigation, legal issuesCoordinate through HR
Discuss with other team membersRumors spread, may tip off subjectNeed-to-know only
Access employee's personal accountsPrivacy violations, inadmissibleFocus on corporate data only
Make assumptions about guiltMay be innocent, could be compromisedInvestigate objectively
Rush to containmentMay alert subject, lose evidencePlan containment carefully with HR/Legal

Step 2: Collect Evidence

// KQL - File access patterns for user
DeviceFileEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(90d)
| where InitiatingProcessAccountName =~ "suspect-user"
| where ActionType in ("FileRead", "FileCopied", "FileModified")
| summarize 
    FileCount = count(),
    UniqueFiles = dcount(FileName),
    DataVolume = sum(FileSize)
    by bin(Timestamp, 1d)
| order by Timestamp asc

// KQL - USB/removable media activity
DeviceEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(90d)
| where DeviceName == "USER-DEVICE"
| where ActionType in ("UsbDriveMounted", "RemovableStorageFileEvent")
| project Timestamp, ActionType, AdditionalFields

// KQL - Cloud upload activity
CloudAppEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(90d)
| where AccountId =~ "suspect-user@company.com"
| where ActionType in ("FileUploaded", "FileShared")
| summarize UploadCount = count(), DataMB = sum(toint(RawEventData.size))/1048576 
    by Application, bin(Timestamp, 1d)

// KQL - Email with attachments to personal/external
EmailEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(90d)
| where SenderFromAddress =~ "suspect-user@company.com"
| where RecipientEmailAddress !endswith "company.com"
| where AttachmentCount > 0
| project Timestamp, RecipientEmailAddress, Subject, AttachmentCount

Containment Actions

Coordinate All Actions with HR/Legal

Unlike external threats, containment of insider threats has employment law implications. Every action must be coordinated.

Escalation Criteria

Immediate Escalation Required When:
  • Trade secrets or critical IP confirmed exfiltrated
  • Data sent to competitor
  • Large-scale PII exposure
  • Evidence of fraud or financial crime
  • Employee is executive or has privileged access
  • Evidence destruction occurring
  • Any indication of criminal activity

Additional Investigation Queries

// KQL - User activity timeline (comprehensive)
let targetUser = "suspect-user@company.com";
let lookback = 90d;
union
    (SigninLogs | where TimeGenerated > ago(lookback) | where UserPrincipalName =~ targetUser 
     | project TimeGenerated, Activity = "SignIn", Details = strcat(AppDisplayName, " from ", IPAddress))),
    (AuditLogs | where TimeGenerated > ago(lookback) | where InitiatedBy.user.userPrincipalName =~ targetUser
     | project TimeGenerated, Activity = "AuditAction", Details = OperationName),
    (CloudAppEvents | where Timestamp > ago(lookback) | where AccountId =~ targetUser
     | project TimeGenerated = Timestamp, Activity = ActionType, Details = strcat(Application, ": ", ObjectName))
| order by TimeGenerated desc

// KQL - Compare user to peer group
let targetUser = "suspect-user";
let targetDept = "Engineering";  // Adjust to user's department
let lookback = 30d;
DeviceFileEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(lookback)
| extend Department = iff(InitiatingProcessAccountName == targetUser, "Target", targetDept)
| summarize FileActions = count(), UniqueFiles = dcount(FileName) 
    by InitiatingProcessAccountName, Department
| order by FileActions desc

// KQL - Sensitive file access
DeviceFileEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(90d)
| where InitiatingProcessAccountName =~ "suspect-user"
| where FolderPath has_any ("confidential", "restricted", "secret", "hr", "finance", "legal", "executive")
| project Timestamp, FileName, FolderPath, ActionType

// KQL - Data movement to external destinations
DeviceNetworkEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(90d)
| where InitiatingProcessAccountName =~ "suspect-user"
| where RemoteUrl has_any ("dropbox", "drive.google", "onedrive.live", "box.com", "wetransfer", "mega.nz")
| summarize Uploads = count(), DataBytes = sum(SentBytes) by RemoteUrl, bin(Timestamp, 1d)
