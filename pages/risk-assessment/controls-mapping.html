Security Controls Mapping

Map security controls across frameworks: NIST CSF, CIS Controls, ISO 27001, and MITRE ATT&CK.

Why Map Controls?

Different frameworks use different terminology for similar concepts. Mapping helps you understand coverage, demonstrate compliance across multiple frameworks, and identify gaps.
