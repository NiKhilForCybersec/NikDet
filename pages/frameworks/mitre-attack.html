MITRE ATT&CK Framework

Comprehensive knowledge base of adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) based on real-world observations.

What is MITRE ATT&CK?

ATT&CK (Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge) is a globally-accessible knowledge base of adversary behavior. It describes WHAT adversaries do (tactics) and HOW they do it (techniques).

Why ATT&CK Matters for SOC
  • Detection Engineering - Map detections to techniques
  • Gap Analysis - Identify coverage gaps
  • Threat Intelligence - Understand adversary behavior
  • Incident Response - Track attack progression
  • Communication - Common language across teams

Detection Example: T1059.001 PowerShell

KQL - Suspicious PowerShell
DeviceProcessEvents
| where TimeGenerated > ago(24h)
| where FileName =~ "powershell.exe" or FileName =~ "pwsh.exe"
| where ProcessCommandLine has_any (
    "-enc", "-encoded", "-e ", 
    "downloadstring", "downloadfile",
    "invoke-expression", "iex",
    "bypass", "-nop", "-w hidden",
    "frombase64string", "decompress"
)
| project TimeGenerated, DeviceName, AccountName,
    ProcessCommandLine, InitiatingProcessFileName

Using ATT&CK in Your SOC

1. Detection Coverage Mapping

Map your existing detections to ATT&CK techniques to identify gaps:

  • Export all detection rules
  • Tag each rule with relevant technique IDs
  • Visualize coverage in ATT&CK Navigator
  • Prioritize gaps based on threat intel

2. Threat-Informed Defense

  • Research threat actors relevant to your industry
  • Identify their common techniques
  • Prioritize detections for those techniques
  • Test with atomic tests or purple team exercises

3. Incident Documentation

  • Tag incidents with ATT&CK techniques
  • Track attack progression through tactics
  • Identify patterns across incidents
  • Report to leadership in common language

ATT&CK Navigator

The ATT&CK Navigator is a web-based tool for visualizing ATT&CK coverage:

  • Create layers - Color-code techniques by coverage
  • Compare - Overlay threat actor TTPs vs your coverage
