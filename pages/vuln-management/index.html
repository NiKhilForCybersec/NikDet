Vulnerability Management

Systematic process to identify, evaluate, prioritize, and remediate security vulnerabilities.

The Vulnerability Management Lifecycle

1
Asset Discovery

Identify all assets in the environment. You can't protect what you don't know exists.

2
Vulnerability Scanning

Regular automated scanning to identify known vulnerabilities.

3
Prioritization

Rank vulnerabilities by risk considering severity, exploitability, and business impact.

4
Remediation

Patch, mitigate, or accept risk based on prioritization.

5
Verification

Rescan to confirm vulnerabilities are resolved.

6
Reporting

Track metrics, communicate risk, demonstrate progress.

CVE Identification

Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE) is the standard identifier:

Format: CVE-YYYY-NNNNN

Example: CVE-2021-44228 (Log4Shell)

Vulnerability Databases

  • NVD (National Vulnerability Database): nvd.nist.gov
  • MITRE CVE: cve.mitre.org
  • CISA KEV: Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog
  • Exploit-DB: exploit-db.com
  • VulnDB: vulndb.cyberriskanalytics.com
