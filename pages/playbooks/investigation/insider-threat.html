PB-INV-011

Insider Threat Investigation

Investigate potential malicious or negligent insider activity including data theft, sabotage, and policy violations.

SeverityHIGH
TierL3/Legal
TimeVariable (days-weeks)
Legal and HR Coordination Required

Insider investigations have legal implications. Always coordinate with HR and Legal before taking action. Document everything meticulously for potential legal proceedings.

Insider Threat Categories

CategoryDescriptionIndicators
Malicious InsiderIntentional harm (theft, sabotage, espionage)Data exfil, unauthorized access, policy bypasses
Negligent InsiderUnintentional harm (carelessness, mistakes)Policy violations, accidental exposure
Compromised InsiderAccount taken over by external attackerUnusual login patterns, impossible travel
Departing EmployeeData theft before leaving organizationMass downloads, USB use, cloud uploads

Part 1: Behavioral Indicators

// KQL: After-hours access patterns
SigninLogs
| where TimeGenerated > ago(30d)
| where UserPrincipalName == "subject@domain.com"
| extend Hour = datetime_part("hour", TimeGenerated)
| extend DayOfWeek = dayofweek(TimeGenerated)
| where Hour < 6 or Hour > 20 or DayOfWeek in (6, 0)  // Off-hours/weekends
| summarize Count = count() by bin(TimeGenerated, 1d), Hour
| order by TimeGenerated desc

// KQL: Access to sensitive resources
AuditLogs
| where TimeGenerated > ago(30d)
| where InitiatedBy.user.userPrincipalName == "subject@domain.com"
| where TargetResources[0].displayName has_any ("HR", "Finance", "Executive", 
    "Confidential", "Restricted", "Legal")
| project TimeGenerated, OperationName, TargetResources[0].displayName

// KQL: Large file downloads
OfficeActivity
| where TimeGenerated > ago(30d)
| where UserId == "subject@domain.com"
| where Operation in ("FileDownloaded", "FileSyncDownloadedFull")
| summarize TotalDownloads = count(), UniqueFiles = dcount(SourceFileName) 
  by bin(TimeGenerated, 1d)
| order by TotalDownloads desc

Part 2: Data Movement Analysis

// KQL: USB device usage
DeviceEvents
| where TimeGenerated > ago(30d)
| where DeviceName in (computers_used_by_subject)
| where ActionType == "PnpDeviceConnected"
| where DeviceDescription has_any ("USB", "Removable", "Mass Storage")
| project TimeGenerated, DeviceName, DeviceId, DeviceDescription

// KQL: Cloud storage uploads
DeviceNetworkEvents
| where TimeGenerated > ago(30d)
| where InitiatingProcessAccountName == "subject"
| where RemoteUrl has_any ("dropbox.com", "drive.google.com", "onedrive.live.com",
    "mega.nz", "box.com", "wetransfer.com")
| summarize TotalBytes = sum(SentBytes) by RemoteUrl, bin(TimeGenerated, 1d)

// KQL: Email to personal accounts
EmailEvents
| where TimeGenerated > ago(30d)
| where SenderFromAddress == "subject@domain.com"
| extend RecipientDomain = tostring(split(RecipientEmailAddress, "@")[1])
| where RecipientDomain in ("gmail.com", "yahoo.com", "hotmail.com", 
    "outlook.com", "protonmail.com")
| where AttachmentCount > 0
| project TimeGenerated, RecipientEmailAddress, Subject, AttachmentCount

Part 3: Access Pattern Analysis

// KQL: Accessing resources outside normal scope
AuditLogs
| where TimeGenerated > ago(30d)
| where InitiatedBy.user.userPrincipalName == "subject@domain.com"
| summarize AccessCount = count() by TargetResources[0].displayName
| join kind=leftanti (
    // Compare to their historical normal access
    AuditLogs
    | where TimeGenerated between (ago(90d) .. ago(30d))
    | where InitiatedBy.user.userPrincipalName == "subject@domain.com"
    | summarize by TargetResources[0].displayName
) on $left.TargetResources_0_displayName == $right.TargetResources_0_displayName
// Result: Resources accessed in last 30 days but NOT in prior 60 days (new access)

// KQL: SharePoint/OneDrive mass access
OfficeActivity
| where TimeGenerated > ago(7d)
| where UserId == "subject@domain.com"
| where Operation in ("FileAccessed", "FileDownloaded")
| summarize 
    UniqueFiles = dcount(SourceFileName),
    UniqueFolders = dcount(SourceRelativeUrl)
  by bin(TimeGenerated, 1h)
| where UniqueFiles > 50  // Unusual volume

Part 5: Evidence Preservation

  • Legal hold: Place email and files on legal hold immediately
  • Forensic image: If warranted, create forensic copy of devices
  • Log preservation: Ensure relevant logs are retained beyond normal retention
  • Chain of custody: Document all evidence handling
  • Screenshots: Capture evidence that may change (rules, settings)

Actions (Coordinate with HR/Legal)

ScenarioAction
Active data theft in progressMay need immediate account disable (coordinate first)
Historical data theftPreserve evidence, assess scope, then action
Negligent behaviorHR to address, training/policy reminder
Departing employee riskEnhanced monitoring, offboarding controls
Important

Never confront the subject directly. This could compromise the investigation and legal proceedings. All communication should go through HR and Legal.
