PB-IR-006

Data Breach Incident Response

Response procedures for confirmed or suspected data breaches involving regulated or sensitive data.

SeverityCRITICAL

Immediate Actions

PriorityActionWho
1Stop the breach - Contain ongoing data lossIR Team
2Preserve evidence - Logs, forensic imagesIR Team
3Notify Legal - Regulatory obligationsIR Lead
4Notify executives - CISO, CEO, General CounselIR Lead
5Engage external counsel - Attorney-client privilegeLegal
6Contact cyber insurance - Early notification requiredLegal/Finance

Scope Assessment Questions

QuestionWhy It Matters
What data was exposed?Determines notification requirements and severity
How many records/individuals?Affects regulatory thresholds and response scale
Who is affected?Customers, employees, partners have different implications
What jurisdictions?Different laws apply (GDPR, CCPA, state laws)
How long was access?Affects scope and impact assessment
Was data encrypted?May affect notification requirements

Notification Requirements

RegulationTimeframeWho to Notify
GDPR72 hoursSupervisory authority, individuals (high risk)
CCPA"Expedient" ~45 daysAffected California residents
HIPAA60 days (individuals)HHS, individuals, media (>500 in state)
State lawsVaries (30-90 days)State AG, affected residents
SEC4 business days8-K filing for material incidents

Data Classification Impact

Data TypeRegulatory Impact
SSN, Financial dataMost state breach notification laws triggered
Healthcare (PHI)HIPAA notification required
EU resident dataGDPR applies regardless of company location
Payment cardsPCI-DSS, card brand notification
Children's dataCOPPA applies, higher scrutiny

External Communications

  • Affected individuals: Clear, helpful notification with next steps
  • Regulators: Timely, complete notification per requirements
  • Media: Prepared statement through PR/communications
  • Partners/vendors: As contractually required
  • Law enforcement: Per policy and legal guidance

Documentation Requirements

  • Breach discovery timeline
  • Scope of affected data and individuals
  • Containment and remediation actions
  • Root cause analysis
  • All notification records and responses
  • Lessons learned and improvements
