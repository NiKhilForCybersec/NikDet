MITRE ATT&CK for Threat Modeling

ATT&CK provides a knowledge base of real adversary behaviors:

  • Identify relevant threat actors: Who targets your industry?
  • Map their techniques: What TTPs do they use?
  • Assess detection coverage: Can you detect those techniques?
  • Prioritize gaps: Focus on techniques used by your threats

Threat Modeling Process

1
Define Scope

What system, application, or environment are you modeling? What are the boundaries?

2
Identify Assets

What are you protecting? Data, functionality, availability?

3
Enumerate Threats

What could go wrong? Use STRIDE, ATT&CK, or brainstorming.

4
Identify Attack Paths

How would an attacker reach the assets? What's the kill chain?

5
Assess Risk

What's the likelihood and impact of each threat?

6
Define Mitigations

What controls address each threat? Prioritize by risk.

Key Questions to Ask

  • What are we building/protecting? (Scope)
  • What can go wrong? (Threats)
  • What are we doing about it? (Mitigations)
  • Did we do a good job? (Validation)
