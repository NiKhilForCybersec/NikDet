Crown Jewels Analysis

Identifying and prioritizing protection of your organization's most critical assets—the data and systems that would cause catastrophic impact if compromised.

What Are Crown Jewels?

Crown jewels are the assets whose compromise would threaten the organization's existence, competitive advantage, or ability to operate. They represent the ultimate targets for sophisticated adversaries.

Common Crown Jewel Categories

CategoryExamplesImpact if Compromised
Intellectual PropertySource code, patents, R&D data, trade secretsLoss of competitive advantage
Customer DataPII, payment data, health recordsRegulatory fines, lawsuits, reputation
Financial SystemsERP, payment processing, bankingDirect financial loss, fraud
Operational TechnologySCADA, ICS, manufacturing systemsSafety, production shutdown
Identity InfrastructureAD, PKI, authentication systemsComplete environment compromise
Strategic InformationM&A plans, board communicationsCompetitive harm, insider trading

Crown Jewels Identification Process

1
Executive Interviews

Ask business leaders: "What keeps you up at night? What would end the business?"

2
Impact Analysis

For each candidate asset, assess: financial impact, operational impact, regulatory impact, reputational impact.

3
Dependency Mapping

Identify all systems, networks, and identities that can access or affect crown jewels.

4
Prioritization

Rank by impact and likelihood to focus protection efforts.

Impact Assessment Questions

Impact AreaQuestions to Ask
FinancialWhat's the direct cost? Lost revenue? Recovery cost?
OperationalCan we continue business? For how long?
RegulatoryWhat fines apply? What notifications required?
ReputationalWould customers leave? Would it make headlines?
LegalWhat lawsuits could result? Contract breaches?
SafetyCould anyone be physically harmed?

Protection Strategies for Crown Jewels

  • Network segmentation: Isolate crown jewels in separate network segments
  • Enhanced monitoring: Higher fidelity detection, longer log retention
  • Privileged access management: Strict controls on who can access
  • Data loss prevention: Monitor and block unauthorized transfers
  • Encryption: At rest and in transit
  • Regular assessment: Penetration testing focused on crown jewels
  • Incident response planning: Specific playbooks for crown jewel incidents

SOC Implications

Crown jewels analysis should drive SOC operations:

  • Alerts involving crown jewels get higher priority
  • Enhanced detection rules for crown jewel access
  • Faster response SLAs for crown jewel incidents
  • Executive notification procedures
  • Regular threat hunting focused on crown jewels
