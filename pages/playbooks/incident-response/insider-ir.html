IR-002

Insider Threat Incident Response

Comprehensive incident response playbook for insider threat incidents. Covers data theft, sabotage, fraud, and policy violations with HR and Legal coordination requirements.

SeverityHigh
TimeDays to Weeks
TeamSOC + HR + Legal
Legal and HR Coordination Required

Insider threat investigations have significant legal and employment law implications. ALL actions must be coordinated with HR and Legal. Maintain strict confidentiality. Evidence must be handled properly for potential legal proceedings or termination.

2.2 Evidence Collection Queries

// KQL - User file access activity
let targetUser = "suspect.user@company.com";
DeviceFileEvents
| where TimeGenerated > ago(90d)
| where InitiatingProcessAccountName =~ targetUser
| where ActionType in ("FileRead", "FileCopied", "FileCreated")
| summarize 
    FileCount = count(),
    TotalSize = sum(FileSize),
    UniqueFiles = dcount(FileName)
    by bin(TimeGenerated, 1d)
| render timechart

// KQL - Sensitive data access
DeviceFileEvents
| where InitiatingProcessAccountName =~ "suspect.user"
| where FolderPath has_any ("Confidential", "Restricted", 
    "HR", "Finance", "Legal", "Executive", "Source")
| project TimeGenerated, FileName, FolderPath, ActionType, FileSize
| order by TimeGenerated desc

// KQL - External transfers
DeviceNetworkEvents
| where InitiatingProcessAccountName =~ "suspect.user"
| where RemoteUrl has_any ("dropbox", "drive.google", 
    "onedrive.live", "wetransfer", "mega.nz")
| where SentBytes > 1048576
| project TimeGenerated, RemoteUrl, SentBytes, InitiatingProcessFileName

// KQL - USB activity
DeviceEvents
| where AccountName =~ "suspect.user"
| where ActionType == "PnpDeviceConnected"
| where DeviceDescription has_any ("USB", "Removable")
| project TimeGenerated, DeviceName, DeviceDescription, DeviceId

// KQL - Email to personal/competitor
EmailEvents
| where SenderFromAddress =~ "suspect.user@company.com"
| where RecipientEmailAddress !endswith "@company.com"
| summarize EmailCount = count(), WithAttachments = countif(AttachmentCount > 0)
    by RecipientEmailAddress
| order by WithAttachments desc

Escalation Criteria

Escalate Immediately If:
  • Confirmed data theft in progress
  • Trade secrets or source code involved
  • Evidence of competitor involvement
  • System sabotage or data destruction
  • Financial fraud detected
  • Multiple insiders colluding
  • Executive-level employee involved
  • Subject becomes aware of investigation
