Containment Decision Making

The Containment Dilemma

Speed vs. Impact

Every containment action has tradeoffs. Faster containment = less damage from attacker, but potentially more business disruption.

Contain Fast If...Investigate More First If...
Active data exfiltrationHistorical alert, attack already over
Ransomware spreadingSingle system, no spread indicators
C2 communication activeBlocked threat, no execution
Critical system compromisedLow-value target, contained impact
Credential theft in progressNeed to understand scope first

Containment Options and Impacts

ActionStopsBusiness ImpactWhen to Use
Isolate endpoint All network communication User can't work, but device preserved Confirmed malware, active threat
Disable account All access for that user User locked out completely Confirmed account compromise
Reset password + revoke sessions Current attacker access User must re-authenticate Credential theft, less disruptive than disable
Block IP/domain Communication to that destination Low if attacker-controlled Known C2, attacker infrastructure
Network segment isolation Spread to/from segment Significant - whole segment offline Worm, fast-spreading threat

L2 Decision Scenarios

ScenarioConsiderationsLikely Action
L1 escalates but you think it's FP L1 might see something you don't. Verify before dismissing. Use as teaching opportunity. Investigate briefly, explain reasoning to L1
Attack happened days ago, just discovered Attacker may have achieved goals. Focus on: Did they persist? What did they access? Full investigation before containment (attacker already got what they wanted)
Executive endpoint compromised High-value target. BEC risk. Sensitive data access. Political sensitivity. Immediate escalation, careful containment with leadership buy-in
Can't determine scope - keep finding more Investigation expanding. Need to balance thoroughness with response speed. Contain what you know, continue investigating, escalate if overwhelming
Business pushes back on containment They have context you don't. But security is your job. Document the risk clearly, escalate to management if needed

When to Escalate to L3/IR

Escalate to L3 or IR Team When:
  • Domain controller or critical infrastructure compromised
  • Evidence of data exfiltration
  • Ransomware deployment (confirmed or imminent)
  • Multiple systems/users compromised (campaign)
  • Can't contain the threat with available tools
  • Nation-state or advanced threat indicators
  • Legal/regulatory implications (breach notification needed)
  • Investigation exceeds your expertise
