Ransomware Incident Response

Critical incident response playbook for ransomware attacks. Covers detection, containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned with specific guidance for different ransomware variants and attack stages.

SeverityCritical
TimeHours to Days
TeamSOC + IR + IT + Legal + Exec
Critical Incident - Immediate Action Required

Ransomware is a time-critical emergency. Every minute of delay allows further encryption and lateral spread. Activate incident response team immediately. Do NOT attempt to negotiate without executive and legal approval. Preserve evidence for potential law enforcement involvement.

Immediate Actions (First 15 Minutes)

1
Activate Incident Response
  • Declare ransomware incident
  • Notify IR team lead and CISO
  • Establish secure communication channel (not corporate email)
  • Begin incident timeline documentation

1.2 Containment Commands

// KQL - Find all systems with ransomware indicators
let ransomwareExtensions = dynamic([".encrypted", ".locked", 
    ".crypto", ".crypt", ".enc", ".pay", ".ransom"]);
DeviceFileEvents
| where TimeGenerated > ago(24h)
| where ActionType == "FileCreated"
| where FileName has_any (ransomwareExtensions)
| summarize 
    EncryptedFiles = count(),
    FirstSeen = min(TimeGenerated)
    by DeviceName
| order by FirstSeen asc

// KQL - Identify potential patient zero
DeviceFileEvents
| where ActionType == "FileCreated"
| where FileName has_any (".encrypted", ".locked", "readme", "ransom")
| summarize FirstEncryption = min(TimeGenerated) by DeviceName
| order by FirstEncryption asc
| take 5

// KQL - Check for lateral movement from patient zero
let patientZero = "HOSTNAME";
let compromiseTime = datetime(2025-01-01T00:00:00Z);
DeviceNetworkEvents
| where TimeGenerated > compromiseTime
| where DeviceName =~ patientZero
| where RemoteIP startswith "10." or RemoteIP startswith "192.168."
| where RemotePort in (445, 135, 139, 3389, 22, 5985)
| summarize Connections = count() by RemoteIP, RemotePort
| order by Connections desc

// PowerShell - Isolate system via firewall
# Block all traffic except management
netsh advfirewall set allprofiles firewallpolicy blockinbound,blockoutbound
netsh advfirewall firewall add rule name="Allow Management" dir=in action=allow remoteip=MANAGEMENT_IP

// PowerShell - Disable SMB to prevent spread
Set-SmbServerConfiguration -EnableSMB1Protocol $false -Force
Set-SmbServerConfiguration -EnableSMB2Protocol $false -Force

2.3 Scope Assessment Queries

// KQL - Full scope of affected systems
DeviceFileEvents
| where TimeGenerated > ago(48h)
| where ActionType == "FileCreated"
| where FileName has_any (".encrypted", ".locked", "ransom", "readme.txt")
| summarize 
    AffectedFiles = count(),
    FirstSeen = min(TimeGenerated),
    LastSeen = max(TimeGenerated)
    by DeviceName
| order by FirstSeen asc

// KQL - Timeline of spread
DeviceFileEvents
| where FileName endswith ".encrypted"
| summarize FirstEncryption = min(TimeGenerated) by DeviceName
| order by FirstEncryption asc
| extend MinutesSinceStart = datetime_diff('minute', FirstEncryption, 
    toscalar(DeviceFileEvents | where FileName endswith ".encrypted" 
    | summarize min(TimeGenerated)))

// KQL - Identify entry vector
let patientZero = "FIRST_AFFECTED_HOST";
let firstEncryption = datetime(2025-01-01T10:00:00Z);
DeviceProcessEvents
| where DeviceName =~ patientZero
| where TimeGenerated between ((firstEncryption - 4h) .. firstEncryption)
| project TimeGenerated, FileName, ProcessCommandLine, 
    InitiatingProcessFileName
| order by TimeGenerated desc

// KQL - Check for precursor malware
DeviceProcessEvents
| where TimeGenerated > ago(7d)
| where FileName has_any ("psexec", "wmic", "cobalt", 
    "mimikatz", "rubeus", "bloodhound")
| project TimeGenerated, DeviceName, FileName, ProcessCommandLine

Escalation Criteria

Escalate to Crisis Management If:
  • Business-critical systems encrypted - ERP, email, file servers
  • Revenue-generating systems affected - production, sales, customer-facing
  • Data exfiltration confirmed - double extortion scenario
  • Recovery time exceeds 24 hours - extended business impact
  • Regulatory data involved - PII, PHI, PCI data encrypted/exfiltrated
  • Ransom demand received - executive decision required
  • Media attention likely - public-facing impact
  • Supply chain impact - partners/customers affected
