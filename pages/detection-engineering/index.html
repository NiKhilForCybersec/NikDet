Detection Engineering

Complete guide to building, testing, and maintaining effective security detections using modern detection-as-code practices.

What is Detection Engineering?

Detection engineering is the discipline of designing, building, testing, and maintaining threat detections. It combines threat intelligence, security operations experience, and software engineering practices.
