Risk Assessment

Methodology for identifying, analyzing, and prioritizing security risks to focus your detection and response efforts.

Why Risk Assessment?

You cannot protect everything equally. Risk assessment helps prioritize security investments based on actual business impact.

Key Principle

Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact

Assessment Process

  1. Asset Discovery - What do we have?
  2. Crown Jewel Analysis - What matters most?
  3. Threat Modeling - Who would attack and how?
  4. Vulnerability Assessment - Where are we weak?
  5. Impact Analysis - What could we lose?
  6. Risk Scoring - Prioritize the risks
  7. Treatment - Mitigate, transfer, accept, avoid
