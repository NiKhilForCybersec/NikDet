Risk Assessment Process

1
Asset Inventory

Discover and catalog all assets. You can't protect what you don't know about.

2
Crown Jewel Identification

Work with business to identify critical assets that would cause significant harm if compromised.

3
Threat Modeling

Identify likely threat actors and their techniques based on industry and assets.

4
Gap Analysis

Map detection coverage against threats. Identify where you can't see attacks.

5
Prioritize & Act

Focus detection development and monitoring on highest-risk combinations.

Common Risk Assessment Mistakes

  • Treating all assets equally: Not all systems deserve the same attention
  • Ignoring business context: Technical severity ≠ business impact
  • Static assessments: Risk changes - reassess regularly
  • No integration: Risk data must flow into SOC tools
  • Over-complication: Start simple, add complexity as needed
