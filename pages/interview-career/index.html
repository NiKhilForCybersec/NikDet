Key One-Liners to Remember

  • On consulting: "I'll learn faster seeing 10 different security programs than staying in one for 10 years."
  • On failure: "I own my failures and create systems to prevent recurrence."
  • On influence: "I influenced by demonstrating, not by demanding."
  • On SIEM: "It depends on the client's ecosystem — there's no universal best."
  • On AI security: "AI is the new shadow IT problem — enable safe adoption, don't just block."
  • On XDR vs SIEM: "Don't duplicate — let each tool do what it's best at."
  • On migration: "Migration is an opportunity to optimize, not just lift-and-shift."
