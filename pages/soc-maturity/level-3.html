Level 3 Success Indicators

  • All alerts triaged within SLA (15 min P1, 4 hours P2)
  • Playbooks exist and followed for all alert types
  • Monthly metrics report delivered to leadership
  • No major incidents discovered by external parties first
  • True positive rate consistently above 50%
  • Staff retention above industry average
  • Regular process improvements implemented

Common Level 3 Challenges

  • Alert fatigue: Even with tuning, volume can overwhelm - focus on quality
  • Shift coverage: Night shift burnout - rotate fairly, compensate appropriately
  • Skill retention: Analysts grow and want to advance - provide growth paths
  • Process drift: Without enforcement, playbooks get ignored
  • Tool sprawl: Resist adding tools without integration plan
