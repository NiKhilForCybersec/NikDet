Risk Assessment Fundamentals

Understanding and quantifying security risks to prioritize defenses and resources.

What is Risk?

Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact

The likelihood of a threat exploiting a vulnerability and the resulting damage

Risk Components

ComponentDefinitionExample
Threat Potential cause of an unwanted incident Ransomware gangs, nation-state actors, insider threats
Vulnerability Weakness that can be exploited Unpatched software, weak passwords, misconfiguration
Impact Consequence if threat is realized Data breach, downtime, financial loss, reputation damage
Likelihood Probability of threat exploiting vulnerability Based on threat capability and motivation

Risk Assessment Process

1
Identify Assets

What do we need to protect? Data, systems, applications, people.

2
Identify Threats

Who might attack? What are their capabilities and motivations?

3
Identify Vulnerabilities

What weaknesses exist? Technical, process, and people vulnerabilities.

4
Assess Likelihood & Impact

How likely is each scenario? What's the potential damage?

5
Prioritize Risks

Rank risks to focus resources on highest priorities.

6
Treat Risks

Mitigate, transfer, accept, or avoid each risk.

Risk Treatment Options

OptionDescriptionWhen to Use
Mitigate Implement controls to reduce likelihood or impact Most common approach for significant risks
Transfer Share risk with third party (insurance, outsourcing) When mitigation is too costly or impractical
Accept Acknowledge risk without action Low risks where cost of mitigation exceeds benefit
Avoid Eliminate the activity causing the risk When risk cannot be reduced to acceptable level

SOC's Role in Risk Management

  • Detection priorities: Focus monitoring on highest-risk assets
  • Alert severity: Calibrate based on asset criticality
  • Response prioritization: Handle incidents affecting critical assets first
  • Coverage decisions: Ensure crown jewels have best detection coverage
  • Reporting: Provide risk context to leadership
