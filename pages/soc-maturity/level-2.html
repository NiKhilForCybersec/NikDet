SOC-CMM Level 2 - Managed

Basic processes established but inconsistently applied.

Level 2 Characteristics

AreaTypical State
ProcessesBasic processes exist but inconsistently followed
ToolsSIEM deployed, basic detection rules
PeopleDedicated security staff, but limited specialization
DetectionSome proactive monitoring, many false positives
ResponseDocumented IR plan, inconsistent execution
MetricsBasic tracking (alert count, incident count)

Common Challenges

  • Alert fatigue from untuned rules
  • Processes exist but aren't always followed
  • Limited coverage of attack techniques
  • Staff stretched across too many responsibilities
  • Training is informal and inconsistent

Improvements to Reach Level 3

  1. Formalize processes: Document and enforce SOPs
  2. Tune detections: Reduce false positives systematically
  3. Training program: Establish formal analyst training
  4. Define tiers: L1/L2/L3 roles with clear responsibilities
  5. Map to framework: Align detections to MITRE ATT&CK
