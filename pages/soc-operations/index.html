Daily SOC Workflow

1
Shift Handoff

Review open incidents, pending escalations, and any ongoing issues from previous shift.

2
Queue Review

Assess alert queue, prioritize by severity, identify any critical items.

3
Alert Triage

Work through queue systematically using playbooks, document findings.

4
Escalation Handling

Process escalated items, coordinate with other tiers as needed.

5
Documentation

Update tickets, document lessons learned, note any detection gaps.

Burnout Prevention

  • Rotate responsibilities: Mix alert triage with projects, hunting
  • Manage alert fatigue: Tune noisy rules, automate repetitive tasks
  • Fair shift scheduling: Distribute weekend/night shifts equitably
  • Career development: Training time, certification support
  • Celebrate wins: Recognize good catches and improvements
  • Post-incident decompression: Time to recover after major incidents
