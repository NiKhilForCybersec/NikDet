Asset Discovery

You can't protect what you don't know exists. Discover and inventory all assets.

Why Asset Discovery Matters

  • Unknown assets can't be monitored - No logs = no detection
  • Shadow IT creates blind spots - Unauthorized systems often unpatched
  • Incident response needs context - Who owns this system? What does it do?
  • Risk assessment requires completeness - Can't assess what you don't know

Asset Attributes to Track

  • Identity: Hostname, IP addresses, MAC address, asset ID
  • Ownership: Business owner, technical owner, department
  • Classification: Criticality, data sensitivity, business function
  • Technical: OS, software versions, open ports, vulnerabilities
  • Network: Location, segment, internet-facing, connections
  • Lifecycle: Provisioned date, last seen, end-of-life

SOC Use of Asset Data

  • Alert enrichment: Add asset context to alerts automatically
  • Prioritization: Incidents on critical assets get higher priority
  • Investigation: Quickly identify asset owner and purpose
  • Coverage analysis: Identify assets not sending logs
  • Vulnerability context: Correlate vulnerabilities with asset criticality
