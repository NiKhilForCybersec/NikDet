SOC Maturity Model

Assess your SOC's current capabilities and build a roadmap for improvement. Know where you are to plan where you're going.

Level 1: Initial

Ad-hoc, reactive security monitoring.

Level 2: Developing

Basic SOC operations established.

Level 3: Defined

Mature processes and consistent operations.

Level 4: Managed

Quantitatively managed with advanced capabilities.

Level 5: Optimizing

World-class, continuously improving operations.

Assessment Best Practices

  • Be honest: Overestimating maturity delays improvement and wastes resources
  • Evidence-based: Require documented proof, not just verbal claims
  • Consistent standards: Apply same criteria across all areas and teams
  • Regular reassessment: Quarterly or semi-annual reviews to track progress
  • Involve stakeholders: Include analysts, managers, and leadership
  • Document gaps: Turn assessment into actionable roadmap with timelines
  • Celebrate progress: Recognize improvements to maintain momentum

Assessment Checklist

  • ☐ Complete self-assessment questionnaire for each dimension
  • ☐ Gather evidence for current state claims
  • ☐ Identify gaps between current and target maturity
  • ☐ Prioritize gaps based on risk and effort
  • ☐ Develop improvement roadmap with timelines
  • ☐ Present to leadership for buy-in and resources
  • ☐ Schedule follow-up assessment date

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing levels: Skipping fundamentals to chase advanced capabilities
  • Tool obsession: Buying tools without people or processes to use them
  • Ignoring culture: Technology can't fix people problems
  • No executive buy-in: Maturity requires sustained investment
