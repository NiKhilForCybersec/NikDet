Escalation Checklist

Always Escalate If:
  • Ransomware detected (any stage)
  • Active attacker/C2 communication
  • Domain controller compromised
  • Credentials confirmed stolen
  • Data exfiltration confirmed
  • Executive accounts targeted
  • Multiple systems affected
  • Production/revenue systems impacted

Universal Triage Questions

  • What? - What exactly happened? What triggered the alert?
  • Who? - Which user, system, or service is involved?
  • When? - When did it start? Is it ongoing?
  • Where? - Source IP, destination, systems affected?
  • How? - What was the method/technique used?
  • Impact? - What's at risk? Data? Systems? Business?
  • Scope? - Is this isolated or widespread?
